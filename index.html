In loving memory

Dr. Harriett Adeline Mattox Hayes

May 21, 1947 to September 27, 2026

My mother built her life around one word: Try.

Dr. Harriett Adeline Mattox Hayes, known to many as “Mama Hayes,” of Greer, South Carolina, passed away peacefully on Sunday, September 27, 2026, at the age of 79.

Born in Millerville, Alabama, on May 21, 1947, to Francis and Billy Ray Mattox, she earned her bachelor’s degree from the University of Montevallo, her master’s from the University of Vermont, and her doctorate from Clemson University. She spent her career as an educator, including her time at Eastside High School.

She raised two boys, and along the way she became a second mother to many of our friends and they loved her dearly.

Throughout her years as a teacher, her advice never changed. Try, and if it doesn’t work, try again. She knew you might fail, and she knew you’d learn something in the process that would probably carry you the next time. Her cup was always half full.

She loved animals and particularly loved watching the ducks and geese play on the lake outside of her kitchen window. After retirement she spent her time hunting for antiques, painting, reading and cooking.

She was preceded in death by her parents. She is survived by her sons, Matthew and Jonathan (Holly), and her granddaughter, Bree, Matt’s daughter.

The family extends a heartfelt thank you to the staff at Prisma Health in Greer, who helped ease her journey with peace and dignity.
